# Surface and card

## Source

- Repeated page compositions in `artifacts/student-college-tracker/src/App.tsx`
  use `rounded-2xl border bg-card` with generous padding.

## Extracted behavior

- Cards group one idea, metric, or action set.
- Surfaces are warm and quiet; hierarchy comes from typography and spacing more
  than heavy shadows.
- Large rounded corners are paired with thin parchment borders.

## Design rules

- Prefer one clear heading and one supporting detail per card.
- Use a stronger border or teal surface only for navigation and high-priority
  emphasis.
- Avoid stacking more than two levels of nested cards.
