# Studywell motion

## Source

- `artifacts/student-college-tracker/src/index.css:109-113`

## Extracted behavior

- The `enter` sequence reveals content with a short upward translation and
  opacity fade.
- Staggered variants add small delays for the second and third content groups.
- The easing is fast at the end, giving the workspace a composed, editorial
  arrival.

## Design rules

- Use motion to establish hierarchy, not to decorate every element.
- Keep reveals short and subtle on productivity surfaces.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences in consuming applications.
